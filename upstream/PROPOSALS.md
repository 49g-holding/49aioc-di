# Proposals upstream

**`[ORG]AIOC-DI` v0.1.0 · 15 October 2026.** Drafts by `[ORG]AIOC-OCO`. Nothing is sent without the governing seat's word. State on 16 October 2026: item 1 is made in the PPD blueprint `v0.4.0`; item 2 in the TOM blueprint `v0.5.0`; item 3 is made in the Source Lifecycle blueprint at its next version.

## 1. To `[ORG]AIOC-PPD`: a release notice to this stage, and its returns

- **Proposed text.** On filing a decided release, PPD also sends a release notice to `[ORG]AIOC-DI`; it receives this AIOC's returns under the Library's design return contract, on a port of its Chief of Staff, and ends each as the contract says.
- **Parts and tests affected.** PPD blueprint §1 card, §6, §17 (two ports on `[ORG]CELL-PCS`); TEST-PPD (new cases).

## 2. To `[ORG]AIOC-TOM`: returns from this stage

- **Proposed text.** TOM receives this AIOC's returns on its elements under the Library's design return contract, as it receives those of PPD.
- **Parts and tests affected.** TOM blueprint §1 card, §6, §17; TEST-TOM; to be made with TOM `v0.5.0`.

## 3. To `[ORG]AIOC-SL`: an origin kind and a filing scope

- **Proposed text.** Add the origin kind `delivery and implementation`, and let `[ORG]AIOC-DI` file sources of that kind and of kind `design report` (its returns), under the same rules as the other design stages.
- **Parts and tests affected.** SL blueprint §5.8; its PRD, at its next opening.

## 4. To `[ORG]AIOC-SL`: change notices and later versions (added with v0.2.0)

- **Proposed text.** When a source is recorded that changes the organisation (a new product, site, market or law), Source Lifecycle sends a notice to `[ORG]AIOC-DI` under its notices (49GAION Protocol §13, item 9), with the change kind. It accepts from `[ORG]AIOC-DI` each later decided version of the living design (a process, procedure, policy, job description or forum) as a filing of origin kind `delivery and implementation`.
- **Parts and tests affected.** SL blueprint §5.8 and its notices; TEST-SL, new cases; DI blueprint §4.3, part 9, and §5.20.
