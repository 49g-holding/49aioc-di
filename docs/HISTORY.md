# History

**`[ORG]AIOC-DI` v0.1.0 · 15 October 2026.**

## Why this stage exists

Process and Position Design ends with a decided design: processes, positions, units, decision rights, controls, forums and workload. A review made on 15 October 2026, by three independent reviewer seats each with its own research, asked whether that is enough to start an organisation working without AI. All three said no. The design says how the organisation should work; it does not bring it into working order on a given day. Still missing: the procedures under each process; the texts the design only names (policies, pay structure, contracts, authority instruments, obligations); the control matrix; the systems and suppliers; the transition plan; and the go-live criteria with the position that decides go or no-go. Leading practice calls this stage implementation, transition or readiness. A few small items went back into Process and Position Design; the rest is this stage.

## Why it stays

Reviews of large change programmes find that most fall short of their goals, and that what separates the ones that hold is a standing rhythm after launch: a transformation office, weekly reviews, a value register, and capability handed over to the organisation. So this stage does not end at go-live. It stays beside the organisation to run the rhythm and improve the processes, and hands its skills over as it goes, so that the organisation never depends on it.

## What was taken from the market

Fifteen products that design, launch or run organisations were taken apart from public material: transformation platforms, organisation-design and process tools, small-business operating systems, implementation and adoption tools. Each useful feature was placed in the stage where it truly belongs. Those that belong here include initiative gates and value tracking, fit to standard and rehearsal scripts, conformance of real work to the design at process level, versioned acknowledgement, runnable checklists, an issue routine, quarterly priorities, an organisation health check, minutes logged only after a preview, and guidance at the point of work. Anything that rates or watches a person, or acts on its own, was not taken.

## Slice 1 first

The product is built in four slices, smallest liability first. The governing seat started slice 1: the gap audit and the launch essentials, reading and proposing only.

## Then complete

On 18 October 2026 the governing seat directed that the stage be completed: the route and the map are written first, and each part is built and switched on in the order of the slices. Version 0.2.0 designs every requirement: the service after launch (accompany, operate, improve), the small business with no design, the ladder with its second level, and records of work read only through a projection that removes who did the work.
