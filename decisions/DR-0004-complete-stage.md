7. **New here, for the mother.** No figure rests on fewer than `min_group` positions; a smaller figure rolls up to the parent unit (Annex H, rule 5). This follows the same rule in the Operations Event Register (49GAION Protocol §22).
8. **Parts.** Three Pods (DI Launch, DI Operations, DI Continuous Improvement) and fourteen AI Cells are added; 34 unit cards and 26 bot identity cards are generated from §17.
