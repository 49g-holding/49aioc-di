# DR-0001 · Delivery and Implementation: a separate AIOC, slice 1 first

- **Date:** 2026-10-15
- **Decided by:** the governing seat (GOV), in conversation with `[ORG]AIOC-OCO`
- **Sources:** the directions of the governing seat of 15 October 2026, translated. On what it is: "An assistant that has all this data, in a user interface; the organisation takes it and delivers it inside itself, and wherever it has a question, it guides it. It diagnoses the implementation, and if a design was made elsewhere, it finds its gaps. After go-live, it optimises and corrects the processes. It monitors the organisation's processes, builds the measures, improves itself, and proposes processes and job descriptions: a mentor. Its input can come from anywhere; the most precise is from the operating model and Process and Position Design. Small and one-person businesses too: it gathers, analyses, advises and fills the gaps itself. A unique product, lasting and strategic." Then, on the product requirements reviewed by three reviewer seats: "Approve it as an AIOC and a part of the Library. Its name stays Delivery and Implementation. Start the first slice." And: "We have started a human process; bring it to its end."
- **Written by:** `[ORG]AIOC-OCO`

## Decision

1. **A separate AIOC.** Delivery and Implementation, `[ORG]AIOC-DI`, is an AIOC of its own, the last stage of the design chain (`49GAION/DR-0017`).
2. **The mother text.** This repository holds the mother blueprint of the Library part 49GAION Delivery and Implementation. It is public and holds no organisation data (Naming Standard R9).
3. **The name** is Delivery and Implementation.
4. **Slice 1 first:** the gap audit and the launch essentials, modes A and B, at permission levels 0 and 1. Slices 2 to 4 follow as later versions, in the order of `PRD-[ORG]AIOC-DI-001` v0.4 unless the governing seat decides otherwise.
5. **It decides nothing** for the organisation; the go or no-go is always the deciding position's.
6. **Processes, not persons.** It addresses positions only, and never ranks, scores or watches the occupant of a position (`49GAION/DR-0015`).

## Not decided here

Everything in blueprint §13; the thresholds and the pricing of `PRD-[ORG]AIOC-DI-001`.
